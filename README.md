# Future Compass AI

Career Coach AI – AI-Powered Career & College Guidance Platform

Create a modern, responsive, student-focused web application called Career Coach AI.

1. Project Overview

Career Coach AI is an AI-powered career guidance and college discovery platform designed mainly for students who have completed 10th or 12th grade and are confused about what to do next.

Many students know that they want to continue studying but are unsure about:

Which career field suits their interests and abilities

Which course or degree they should choose

Which career has better opportunities for them

Which skills they need to develop

Which colleges offer the desired course

Which college is suitable according to their budget, location and preferences

What the teaching environment and faculty of a college are actually like

Career Coach AI should help students discover, explore, compare and make informed career and college decisions.

The platform should not make decisions on behalf of students. Instead, it should provide personalized recommendations, comparisons and information so the student can make the final decision.

2. Main User Flow

The application should follow this journey:

Student Profile → Interest Assessment → AI Career Analysis → Career Recommendations → Career Roadmap → Course Finder → College Finder → Faculty & Teaching Preview → College Comparison → Personalized Decision

20. Important Ethical & Data Requirements

The application must clearly communicate:

AI recommendations are guidance, not guaranteed predictions.

College information should be verified before making admission decisions.

Fees, placements, faculty information and admission rules may change.

Students should verify important information through official college/university sources.

Do not fabricate college data, faculty credentials or placement statistics.

Clearly distinguish verified information from AI-generated recommendations.

Protect student profile information and avoid unnecessary personal data collection.
